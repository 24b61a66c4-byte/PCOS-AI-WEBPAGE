⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

Important information about using this health tool

⚠️ NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR MEDICAL ADVICE

The PCOS Smart Assistant is an educational and informational tool only. It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for any medical concerns or before making health decisions.

1. Purpose of This Application

The PCOS Smart Assistant is designed to:

  • Help you track menstrual cycles and symptoms
  • Provide educational information about PCOS and women's health
  • Generate risk assessments based on anonymized medical datasets
  • Offer general lifestyle recommendations
  • Facilitate health conversations with your doctor

This application is NOT intended to:

  • Diagnose PCOS or any medical condition
  • Replace professional medical examination
  • Prescribe treatment or medication
  • Provide emergency medical services

2. Risk Assessment Limitations

Risk scores are educational estimates only.

Risk assessments are generated using statistical algorithms and public medical datasets. They do NOT represent a clinical diagnosis and should NOT be used as the sole basis for health decisions.

Important limitations:

  • Algorithms cannot account for individual medical history
  • Self-reported data may be incomplete or inaccurate
  • Results do not consider genetic factors, medications, or other conditions
  • Risk scores are not validated for clinical diagnostic use

3. AI Chat Assistant Limitations

The AI health assistant uses artificial intelligence to answer questions. You should know:

  • AI responses are generated by algorithms, not medical professionals
  • AI may provide incorrect, outdated, or incomplete information
  • AI cannot interpret your specific medical situation
  • AI responses are for educational purposes only
  • AI should NOT be relied upon for medical decisions

Never use AI chat for medical emergencies. Call emergency services immediately if you have a medical emergency.

4. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Use of this application does NOT create a doctor-patient relationship. The creators, developers, and maintainers of this application:

  • Are NOT your healthcare providers
  • Do NOT provide personalized medical advice
  • Have NO access to your complete medical history
  • Cannot and do NOT monitor your health status

5. When to Seek Medical Care

Consult a healthcare provider immediately if you experience:

  • Severe pelvic pain or cramping
  • Heavy bleeding (soaking through a pad/tampon every hour)
  • Bleeding for more than 7 days
  • Sudden changes in menstrual patterns
  • Fever along with pelvic pain
  • Dizziness or fainting
  • Any symptoms that concern you

For emergencies: Call your local emergency number (911 in the US) or go to the nearest emergency room.
