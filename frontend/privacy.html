Privacy Policy

How we protect your health information

🔒 Your Privacy is Our Priority

Your health data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, store, or share your personal health information on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Health Data (Stored Locally)

When you use the PCOS Smart Assistant, the following information is stored only on your device using browser localStorage:

  • Personal information (age, weight, height)
  • Menstrual cycle data (cycle length, period length, last period date)
  • Symptoms and health indicators
  • Lifestyle information (stress, sleep, exercise)
  • Clinical measurements (if provided)

1.2 Anonymous Analytics (Optional)

With your consent, we may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the application:

  • Page views and feature usage
  • Error logs (without personal identifiers)
  • Performance metrics

Note: These analytics do NOT include any health data.

1.3 AI Chat Interactions

When you use the AI health assistant:

  • Your chat messages may be processed by third-party AI providers (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Perplexity)
  • We anonymize queries and do not send personally identifiable health data
  • Conversations are not stored permanently

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Local Processing

All health analysis and risk assessment calculations are performed on your device. Your data never leaves your browser unless you explicitly choose to:

  • Use the AI chat feature
  • Export your data
  • Enable cloud backup (if available)

2.2 AI Services

When you interact with the AI health assistant:

  • Only your chat questions are sent to AI providers
  • No identifying information is included
  • Previous health data entered in forms is NOT sent to AI services

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

  • Data is stored in your browser's localStorage
  • Data remains on your device until you clear browser data or delete it manually
  • No one else can access this data (unless they have physical access to your device)

3.2 Security Measures

  • HTTPS encryption for all web communications
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers
  • No server-side storage of health data
  • Regular security audits

3.3 Optional Cloud Backup (If Enabled)

If you choose to enable Supabase cloud backup:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Data is stored with row-level security (RLS)
  • You can delete your cloud data at any time
  • Backup is optional and OFF by default

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 AI Providers

AI chat functionality uses:

  • OpenRouter: Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI: Privacy Policy
  • Perplexity: Privacy Policy

4.2 Hosting and Infrastructure

  • Vercel: Application hosting - Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (Optional): Cloud backup - Privacy Policy

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your stored data anytime
  • Export: Download your data in JSON/CSV format
  • Delete: Clear all data from your device instantly
  • Modify: Edit or update any information

5.2 AI Chat Opt-Out

You can choose not to use the AI chat feature. The core health analysis functions work without AI.

5.3 Analytics Opt-Out

You can disable anonymous analytics in the settings (if enabled).

6. Children's Privacy

This service is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Displaying a notice on the application
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date below

Continued use of the service after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact:

  • GitHub: PCOS-AI-WEBPAGE Repository
  • Issues: Open an issue on GitHub for privacy concerns
